STOCK BOOK — SMART RETAIL INTELLIGENCE r4

OPEN
Main Stock Book > Intelligence > Smart Retail Intelligence

CONTROLS
- Choose the analysis period: 30, 60 or 90 days.
- Use the global store selector or page-level branch selector to scope product stock and product-level recommendations.
- Click product names to open existing product details and movement history.
- Click Review on a transfer suggestion to open the existing transfer form with suggested source, destination and quantity prefilled. Review all details before saving.
- Download Excel to export the summary and analysis lists.

WHAT EACH AREA MEANS
- Today's action center: high-level issues found in current saved data.
- Product Health: transparent heuristic score based on recent net sales, estimated stock cover, last sale recency and returns.
- Slow stock: positive stock with no positive sale in the selected period. Last sale date is all-time where history exists.
- Restock watch: positive recent demand with 7 or fewer estimated days of net-sales cover; quantity aims for about 21 days.
- Suggested transfers: a receiving branch has demand and under 7 days of cover, while another branch appears to hold stock above an estimated 21-day buffer.
- Branch performance: saved net/gross unit sales and returns alongside current Stock Book quantity. These are units, not revenue or profit.
- Stock value: latest available BILLZ supply price multiplied by current Stock Book on-hand quantity; incomplete price coverage is reported.
- Physical stock count: download the workbook template, enter physical counts, upload the workbook, review discrepancies and export the difference list. No stock is changed by this tool.

DATA QUALITY
All insights rely on the movement rows synchronized and matched into Stock Book. Deleted, free and unmatched records are excluded from selected sales insights. Missing BILLZ sales, incorrect SKU/variant mapping, incomplete historical records or mismatched store names can affect results. Always run BILLZ full sync and Compare stock with BILLZ first.

SAFE OPERATION
No automatic transfers, replenishment orders, price changes or stock adjustments are made by Smart Retail Intelligence. Existing transaction workflows remain the way to apply approved changes.
