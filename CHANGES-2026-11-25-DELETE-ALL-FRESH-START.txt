NEW BUTTON: BILLZ > Advanced BILLZ tools > "DELETE ALL DATA and start fresh from BILLZ"

1. Downloads a backup JSON first (stock-book-full-backup-before-delete-all-DATE.json). Keep it.
2. PERMANENTLY deletes every document in the Firestore "moves" collection: imports, sales, transfers, write-offs,
   product catalog (billzmeta*, billzvariants*), photos, sync status. Nothing goes to the Deleted tab.
   Kept on purpose: auth_revoke_* (login-session logout markers) and billz_auto_lock (auto-sync lock).
3. Clears the copy stored in this browser (IndexedDB stockbook-local-cache-v1). This copy is why products
   kept showing after moves was deleted in the Firebase console: the app shows the last saved local copy first.
4. Loads the complete BILLZ catalog and categories again from scratch.
5. Syncs imports, transfers, write-offs and sales from the chosen start date to today, 31 days at a time.

Confirm word: DELETE ALL.  If deleting says permission-denied, the Firebase rules block deletes from the app.
Other devices/browsers keep their own local copy until they open the app once and receive the empty database
(or clear site data).
Limits: stock = BILLZ imports - sales +/- transfers from the start date. Pieces that never came in as a BILLZ import
are missing. Not tested against live Firebase/BILLZ.
