WARDA STOCK BOOK — BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE & THEME UPDATE
Date: 25 November 2026

MAIN SYSTEM LOCATION
Open the main Stock Book website and choose Analytics in the left navigation.

FOUR ANALYTICS PILLARS
1. Sales Performance
   Uses BILLZ management analytics for net sales revenue, units sold, returns,
   daily revenue trend, store performance and salesperson performance.

2. Product Intelligence
   Connects to the existing Smart Retail Intelligence page for product health,
   slow-moving products, restock watch, transfer suggestions, color/size demand,
   and physical stock-count audits.

3. Financial Intelligence
   Uses BILLZ seller/payment reports for gross profit when returned by BILLZ,
   cash/card/other payments, discounts, returns and revenue. If BILLZ does not
   return a field, the interface shows it as unavailable rather than inventing it.

4. Market Intelligence
   Adds an internal demand-trend view: category sales compared with the previous
   equal-length period, current stock exposure and leading/growing categories.
   IMPORTANT: This is Warda's own sales/stock signal, not an external competitor
   market scan. Competitor prices, external market share and third-party trend
   feeds are not connected in this release.

CATEGORY BREAKDOWN
The Analytics page separates Clothing, Shoes, Accessories, Cosmetics, Hair and
Other. Classification uses BILLZ/Stock Book category and product-name metadata.
Rows show sold units, returns, net units, current stock, products, products
without recorded sales and change versus the previous equal-length period.
Category revenue is intentionally not estimated or fabricated when BILLZ does
not supply a reliable category-level financial breakdown.

THEME MANAGER
Open Style at the bottom of the left navigation.
New professional presets:
- Analytics Light (new default)
- Analytics Dark
- Finance
- Retail BI
Existing presets remain available, including Indigo, Samarkand, Graphite,
Midnight, Linen, Rose, Sunset, Ocean, Forest, Plum, Mocha, Mint, Sky, Lavender,
Gold, Aurora, Obsidian and Pearl.
A user can also upload a screenshot/image or a theme JSON palette. The theme is
applied immediately and saved in that browser; changing the theme does not need
another deployment. Theme preference is browser-specific in this release.

DATA / AI LIMITS
- Category metrics are based on movements already synchronized/saved in Stock Book.
- Market signals are internal demand analysis only. External competitor research
  needs trusted external data sources or user-provided competitor datasets.
- This release does not activate a live generative-AI provider. Rule-based
  demand signals and existing stock recommendations are not described as AI.
  A real AI advisor requires a server-side provider key and a secure API route.
- Financial category splits are not invented when the BILLZ endpoint does not
  provide category-level values.

TESTING
The updated inline application script was syntax-checked with Node.js.
Live BILLZ, Firebase, Supabase and Telegram integrations still require staging
verification with the owner's credentials and real test dates/stores.
